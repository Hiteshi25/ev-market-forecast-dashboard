# Global EV Market Forecast Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing global electric vehicle (EV) market growth, adoption, stock, charging infrastructure, market trends, forecasts, and policy insights.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze historical global EV market data and present meaningful insights about EV adoption, sales growth, EV stock, charging infrastructure, major markets, and future market outlook.

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Python
- Pandas
- Data Visualization
- Microsoft Bing Maps / Azure Maps

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of:
- Global EV sales
- EV stock
- EV sales share
- Charging infrastructure
- Global EV sales trends
- Major market comparison

### 2. Market Analysis
Provides regional and market-level analysis including:
- EV sales comparison
- EV stock comparison
- EV sales share by market
- Top EV markets
- Charging infrastructure growth
- Country/region filtering

### 3. Technology & Infrastructure
Focuses on:
- EV sales by powertrain
- BEV, EV, FCEV and PHEV trends
- Charging infrastructure
- EV sales share
- Technology and infrastructure growth

### 4. Forecast & Outlook
Analyzes:
- Historical EV sales
- Future EV sales forecast
- EV adoption trend
- Market opportunity
- Top markets
- Year-over-year EV sales expansion

### 5. Geographic & Policy Insights
Provides:
- Global geographic distribution of EV sales
- EV sales by region/country
- Key EV policy drivers
- Major government policy measures
- Policy impact and sources

## Key Insights

The dashboard highlights the rapid growth of the global EV market, increasing EV adoption, expansion of charging infrastructure, differences between major markets, and the role of government policies in supporting EV adoption.

## Data

The dashboard uses EV market data covering historical trends from 2010–2025, with forecasting extending toward 2035.
